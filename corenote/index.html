CoreNote

Notes on floating cards, plus where you signed up.

Each note is a floating card. Four canvases: Amber, White, Slate, and Pine. Accounts is a list of an email or phone number and the app names you registered with it — names only, never a password. Deleted notes stay in Trash for 7 days. Notes sync through your own private iCloud. No account, no ads, and no server of ours.
