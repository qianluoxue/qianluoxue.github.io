CoreNote

Smart floating card notes, synced through your iCloud.

Each note is a floating card across four canvases: Amber, White, Slate, and Pine. Deleted notes stay in Trash for 7 days. Notes sync through your own private iCloud. No account and no ads; optional Cloud AI features are off by default and run through a stateless relay only when you enable them.
