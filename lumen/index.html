Lumen Mail

Private IMAP mail client with an optional AI inbox secretary.

Lumen connects directly from your device to iCloud, Gmail, QQ Mail, NetEase Mail, Fastmail, Yahoo, or any standard IMAP/SMTP mailbox. Credentials stay in the system Keychain, and remote tracking images are blocked. On-device translation and summaries stay local; optional Cloud AI Secretary features are off by default and use a stateless relay only when enabled.
